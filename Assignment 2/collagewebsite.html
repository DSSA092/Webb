About the Department

The Department of Computer Science and Engineering introduces students to computing, software development, data systems and emerging technologies. It encourages problem-solving, teamwork, practical learning and responsible use of technology.

Our aim: to develop graduates with strong technical foundations and the ability to apply computing concepts to real-world problems.
